The Network

Built for serious professionals

Friends of Medicine members are verified healthcare professionals and business leaders - physicians, nurses, advanced practitioners, founders, investors, and the specialists who build alongside them.

Every activation is intentionally curated, intimate, and premium. This is not a conference. This is a community.

Sponsoring FOM places your brand directly in the hands of decision-makers who influence healthcare purchasing, business growth, and community trust - at both a local and national level.

Why Partner With Us

What partnership looks like

Four core benefits that compound over the course of a sponsorship year - whether you align with a single event, a city, or the full national footprint.

01 / Presence

Brand Visibility at Events

Premium placement across in-person activations, signage, digital displays, and event materials - reaching a verified, high-caliber professional audience every time you show up.

02 / Access

Direct Line to a Premier Network

Meaningful exposure to an elite community of healthcare professionals and business leaders across multiple U.S. markets - the rooms most brands spend years trying to enter.

03 / Voice

Speaking & Presentation Opportunities

Present your brand story, product, or mission directly to a captive audience of influential professionals - in moments designed for attention, not distraction.

04 / Amplification

Social & Digital Exposure

Co-branded content, social features, and digital promotion to FOM's engaged community - before, during, and after every event. The room doesn't end when the night does.

The Experience

Four signature series, every month

FOM runs four distinct monthly activations, each designed to bring a different dimension of the network together - from wellness and innovation to entrepreneurship and connection. Sponsors gain recurring visibility across every touchpoint throughout the year.
