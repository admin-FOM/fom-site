Why The Network Exists

Proximity creates opportunity.

Friends of Medicine is a curated community of healthcare professionals, founders, and the people who serve them. We believe the right room changes everything: the introduction that becomes a partnership, the conversation that becomes a company, the table you did not know existed until someone pulled out a chair. The Network exists to put you in that room, deliberately and on your terms.

What began in Miami is becoming national. The Network is expanding through 2026 across Fort Lauderdale, West Palm Beach, Dallas, New York, and Atlanta, with Miami as the flagship. Wherever you are, the people worth knowing are closer than you think.

What the Portal Gives You

Membership is a tool, not a token.

Inside the Portal, your membership goes to work. Build a profile that tells The Network who you are. Explore The Room, the members-only space where the community lives, and discover who else is part of it. Declare what you are looking for, or offer what you do, and let the Portal quietly surface the right people to meet it.

Connection here is intentional by design. Your identity and contact details stay private until both sides agree to talk. Visibility comes first. Access comes only with consent. The result is a network where every introduction means something.

  • A profile in The Room, the members-only space where the community gathers
  • Discovery of members across The Network
  • The ability to declare a need or offer your services and receive curated introductions
  • Private, consent-based connections and messaging
