City Partner Program

Build the room in your city

Friends of Medicine is looking for locally respected operators to steward our presence in new markets. Not promoters. Not event producers. Stewards of community.

The Thesis

HQ provides brand, systems, and support. Local momentum must be created locally.

From the FOM City Partner Framework
The Structure

Two roles. One progression.

Every FOM city operates with a Partner and a team of up to five Ambassadors. Ambassadors learn from the inside - and earn consideration for a Partner role as the network expands.

Entry Point
City Ambassador
Up to 5 per city · Apprenticeship

An unpaid apprenticeship. Work alongside the City Partner, learn the playbook from the inside, activate your network, and prove the judgment a Partner role requires.

Earn the role. Earn the city.
The Destination
City Partner
1 per city · Stewardship

The steward of FOM's presence in the city. Responsible for filling the room, upholding brand standards, and representing FOM within the local healthcare community.

Own the city. Build it right.

Most City Partners begin as Ambassadors. Strong track records in the Ambassador role earn consideration when new markets open - in your city, or adjacent ones.

The City Partner Role

Stewardship, presence, and community

City Partners represent the tone and integrity of Friends of Medicine in their market. They generate local attendance through relationship-based outreach and uphold the quality of every activation.

What the role is

Relationship-based leadership

  • Represent FOM at monthly activations and key gatherings
  • Own local attendance - fill the room through your network
  • Drive local ticket, Inner Circle, and sponsorship growth
  • Uphold brand presence, tone, and standards
  • Activate and coordinate your team of City Ambassadors
  • Serve as the point of connection for the network and guests
  • Provide local insight that sharpens program quality
  • Promote across approved channels - LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp, email
What the role is not

What HQ handles centrally

  • Negotiating with sponsors independently
  • Setting partnership, compensation, or pricing terms
  • Event production logistics outside agreed scope
  • Sponsor access and partnership strategy
  • National financial arrangements and economic decisions
  • Setting brand guardrails or Inner Circle vetting criteria
The City Ambassador Role

The apprenticeship path

A select team of up to five Ambassadors work alongside each City Partner. It's an unpaid role - and an intentional one. The reward isn't money. It's the judgment, relationships, and operational insight you earn by being in the room.

01 / Content

Content Capture

Capture photo and video during events. Upload to the city group chat for the media team to use across FOM's channels.

02 / Promotion

Network Activation

Share event promotion with your professional circles. Extend FOM's reach through people who trust your judgment.

03 / Presence

Event Night Engagement

Welcome guests, facilitate introductions, and help the Partner maintain the energy and flow of the room.

04 / Growth

Network Introduction

Introduce qualified attendees to FOM. Connect the right people to the right path.

05 / Standards

Culture Protection

Uphold FOM values in every interaction. Professionalism, discretion, and sound judgment - always.

06 / Apprenticeship

Learn The Playbook

Observe how a city runs. Understand what works. Absorb the operational knowledge that makes a future Partner.

What Ambassadors Get

Access, exposure, and a path forward

  • Free entry to all FOM events in your city
  • Access to select partner events
  • Direct mentorship from your City Partner
  • Exposure to FOM HQ leadership and network
  • Proximity to physicians, operators, founders, investors
  • Demonstrated path to City Partner consideration
The Honest Offer

Not paid. Intentionally.

  • No salary, commission, or per-event bonus
  • Ambassador is an apprenticeship, not a job
  • Time commitment is real - plan accordingly
  • Appointments are approved by HQ, not open-enrollment
  • The role selects for people playing the long game
  • The compensation is the network, the access, the path
Why This Works The Ambassador role exists to identify and develop future City Partners. We're not looking for people who need a side hustle - we're looking for people who see the bigger picture and are willing to earn their seat. If that sounds right, apply.

The Process

Four steps from application to onboarding

We move deliberately. The right partner is worth waiting for.

01

Application

Fill out the form. Tell us about your city, your network, and why you want to steward FOM's presence there.

02

Discovery Call

A 30-minute conversation with the FOM team. Learn about the role in depth. We learn whether there's fit.

03

Alignment

If both sides see the potential, we move to alignment - scope, expectations, terms discussed individually.

04

Onboarding

Brand systems, playbook, direct line to FOM HQ. You begin activating your city and building your Ambassador team.
