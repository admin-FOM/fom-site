The Reset Retreat

A single weekend each year. Set apart for the Inner Circle and select high-performance guests.

By Invitation · Inner Circle Access
The Philosophy

Why we reset.

The pace of medicine, capital, and innovation doesn't slow. The schedules don't either. Once a year, Friends of Medicine gathers a small group - the Inner Circle and a handful of high-performance guests - for a weekend designed to interrupt the rhythm.

To reset. To return to the work with a different center. To be in a room with people who understand the speed you usually live at - and what it costs.

The Weekend, Sketched

A different rhythm.

The agenda is curated, never published. The shape is the same each year, the substance designed for the room that gathers.

Wellness

Movement, recovery, and stillness designed by practitioners who understand what high performers actually need - not what looks good on a brochure.

Strategy

Curated conversations on what comes next - in practice, in business, in the work you came here to do. Not panels. Not pitches. Conversations.

Connection

The kinds of conversations that don't happen at 8AM rounds or Q4 board meetings. The ones that happen when phones are down and schedules are off.

The Room

Who's in.

The Inner Circle, from every chapter. A small number of select high-performance guests. No press. No media. No livestreams.

Phones down. Schedules off. Real attendance.

A Seat Begins With Inner Circle

The room is built from the network.

The annual Reset Retreat is part of Inner Circle access. There is no separate registration. The Inner Circle decides who's in the room - and the conversation starts long before the weekend does.
