A Week In The Network

Four nights. One network.

Each event brings a different dimension of the room together. This is the cadence the network lives by.

Tuesday · Tech

Tech founders in the room.

Healthcare technology, AI, and the operators building what's next. A monthly evening for the people designing tomorrow's clinical infrastructure.

Wednesday · Wellness

Wellness, by the water.

Longevity, performance, and the practitioners who treat physicians and executives. Slower pace, deeper conversation.

Thursday · Unwind

An unhurried night.

Premium spirits. No pitches. The night the network simply gets to be in the same room - without an agenda.

Friday · Founders

Founders Friday - invite-only.

The most selective night of the month. Operators, investors, and decision-makers behind closed doors. Access by invitation.

And that's just the start of the month.

Beyond The Door

What Inner Circle unlocks as the network grows.

Some doors open today. Others open as the room gets stronger. The roadmap belongs to Inner Circle.

01 / 09

A Private Inner Circle Thread

An Inner Circle-only WhatsApp community with city-specific and national channels. Direct peer access across healthcare, business, technology, and entrepreneurship.

02 / 09

CME Dinners

Curated continuing-education evenings by specialty - clinical innovation, industry shifts, and the conversations that don't happen at conferences.

03 / 09

Annual Recognition Dinner

A black-tie, fully-sponsored evening in the flagship city. Inner Circle members from every chapter, each with a guest. A night to honor the people building the network.

04 / 09

Speaking & Podcast Visibility

Priority consideration for podcast guest appearances and curated speaking opportunities. The platform amplifies the members.

05 / 09

Sponsorship & Partnership Pipeline

Strategic visibility with healthcare leaders across markets. Eligibility to co-host, partner, and present - subject to review and brand alignment.

06 / 09

First-In-Line For Retreats

Early access to The Reset Retreat, summits, and invite-only experiences. First consideration for limited-capacity programs.

07 / 09

A Welcome In Every City

Travel into Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Dallas, New York, or Atlanta? An Inner Circle member meets you there. Proximity, in motion.

08 / 09

Media & Editorial Features

Eligibility to be featured across Friends of Medicine platforms - editorial profiles, member spotlights, and press collaborations as the network grows.

09 / 09

The Things We Don't Announce

Some doors only open from the inside. The unscheduled introductions. The dinners that never appear on a calendar. Inner Circle is the ticket past the program.
